The problem we are solving

A process scattered across tabs, documents, and dates.

Canadian immigration guidance is extensive, and each pathway follows its own sequence. Applicants often piece together official pages, personal notes, spreadsheets, forum posts, and calendar reminders just to maintain a working picture of their case.

MaplePath brings that picture into one place. A selected pathway becomes a structured roadmap with preparation guidance, document lists, official references, editable dates, and personal progress.

Product principles

Built to earn clarity, not assume it.

MaplePath is still growing. These principles guide how features and pathway information are added.

◎

Official sources first

Roadmaps point users back to government guidance and display when program information was reviewed.

◇

Privacy by restraint

MaplePath asks for the information needed to organize a journey, without requesting immigration document uploads.

↗

Community data with context

Timeline averages stay separated by immigration route so different programs are not compared as if they were the same.

+

Useful before predictive

Reliable tracking and clean data come first. Better analytics and responsible predictions can grow from that foundation.
