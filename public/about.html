MaplePath helps immigrants navigate the uncertainty of Canadian immigration by providing personalized progress tracking and community-driven timeline estimates. Our mission is to make the immigration process more transparent, organized, and less stressful for everyone.

The first release focuses on the Manitoba Provincial Nominee Program (MPNP), with support for additional immigration pathways planned in future updates.
