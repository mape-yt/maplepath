Founder & developer

Hi, I’m Seungyun. I’m building the tool I wish my family had.

MaplePath began with a personal question: how can technology make a family’s immigration journey feel more understandable and less overwhelming?

THE STARTING POINT

One lived experience.
One practical idea.

My own family’s immigration journey to Canada showed me how much work happens outside the application itself. There are requirements to interpret, documents to coordinate, waiting periods to understand, and dozens of small decisions that are easy to lose track of.

I started MaplePath to turn that uncertainty into something visible: a roadmap that tells you where you are, what to prepare, and what comes next. The product combines my interest in software with a problem that is deeply personal to me.

MaplePath is being built step by step. The current platform supports user accounts, pathway-specific roadmaps, editable timelines, community averages, and official source references. The longer-term goal is to use carefully structured anonymous data to make immigration timelines more transparent and useful.
