Privacy notice

How MaplePath uses your information.

Updated November 8, 2026. Questions? Contact the MaplePath project at sylim.cs@gmail.com.

What you choose to save

We store your username and a password hash, your immigration profile and selected pathways, tasks, application details, journey progress, and any timeline dates you enter. An optional recovery email and temporary verification or reset records support account recovery. Dates can be left blank. Please do not put sensitive identifiers or documents in task titles.

Why we use it

Your information lets us sign you in, show your roadmap, save progress, and help you recover your account. Eligible self-reported timelines can contribute to community comparisons described in our community method. Existing test and unlabelled data is excluded. We do not sell your information or use account emails for marketing.

Community comparisons

Public-facing comparisons do not display usernames or individual timelines. Saved timelines are still linked to your account in our database; they are not anonymous records internally. Small groups are withheld, but aggregated statistics cannot guarantee anonymity. We do not import online personal stories into community data.

Cookies and your device

An essential session cookie keeps you signed in for up to seven days. It is protected from JavaScript and sent over HTTPS on the live site. Your browser also stores username and journey-type hints for the interface. MaplePath does not currently include advertising trackers or third-party visitor analytics.

Services that process information

Render hosts the site and MongoDB Atlas stores account data. These services can process operational data such as connection details and logs. When account email is enabled, the configured email service (Brevo or Resend) processes the recipient address, message, and delivery details. Contact messages go to our Gmail inbox. Provider processing and storage may occur outside Canada; we have not promised Canada-only storage.

Keeping and deleting data

Account information stays while your account exists. You can edit profile and timeline details, or permanently delete your account in Account settings. Deletion removes the profile, tasks, applications, timeline records, and sessions from the active database. Subsequent community calculations no longer include those timelines. A comparison already loaded in another browser will update on refresh.

Account deletion does not erase emails you sent to our support inbox or provider operational logs. Contact us about those copies. Temporary recovery links expire after 30 minutes. Encrypted local backups may contain older copies. We keep up to seven successful backups; older copies are removed when replaced. Backups currently run manually, so this is a count limit, not a fixed number of days. A local restore has been tested. Deletions must be reconciled before any backup is returned to service.

Your choices and requests

You can use the site without a recovery email, but forgotten-password recovery requires an address you previously verified. Contact sylim.cs@gmail.com to request access to your information, corrections, or help with deletion. We may need to verify account ownership; we will never ask for your password or reset token by email.

Changes to this notice

We will update this page when data handling changes. Any future use of personal data for a new purpose needs a separate review and, where required, your consent.
